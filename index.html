🗺️ The Plan

Here's what we get up to

Tap any week to see what it's about.

💛 Come to as many weeks as you like — drop in any time. You never have to have been here before. Every level of English is welcome: answer in one word or a whole story. And you can always say “pass.”

Week 10 is a showcase. Performing is 100% optional.

Some people do skits, songs, stories or comedy. Others watch, help, and cheer everyone on. Both are the whole point.

💛 "I'm just here to talk and have fun" — that counts. You belong here.
